## 2. Woran hat noch niemand gedacht? (≥ 3 werden eingebaut)
1. Farb-Etikett je Raum + QR auf jedem Karton: neue Wohnung bekommt dieselben Farbpunkte an den Türen, Team trägt ohne Fragen; QR zeigt Packliste.
2. „Erste-Nacht-Karton“: zuletzt rein, zuerst raus (Bettzeug, Ladekabel, Kaffee, Klopapier, Werkzeug), Liste zum Abhaken.
3. Fristen-Rechner: Umzugsdatum eingeben → Halteverbot spätestens beantragen, Schilder stehen 3 volle Tage vorher (BVerwG 24.05.2018, 3 C 25.16), Ummelden binnen 14 Tagen (§ 17 BMG) als Kalender-Datei.
4. Trageweg-Rechner: Etage, Aufzug, Laufweg in m → ab wann Möbellift günstiger ist.
5. Stauplan: Kunde sieht, wo im LKW sein empfindlichstes Stück steht (oben, gesichert, gepolstert).

## 3. Drei Leitideen (je eigene freie Farbwelt)
- SB1 „Ein Karton reist mit“ – Kraftpapier/Creme #efe6d6 + Bordeaux #7a1f2e. Ein beschrifteter Karton ist der Held, Kamera folgt ihm von der alten Küche bis ins neue Regal; jede Station = Haltepunkt mit Unterseite. Match-Cuts über den Karton.
- SB2 „Abreißkalender – noch 42 Tage“ – Aubergine #2a1630 + Mint #7fe0c0. Scrollen reißt Kalenderblätter ab (T-42 → Einzug → +14 Ummelden), jedes Blatt gibt ein Foto der Phase frei.
- SB3 „Vogelperspektive“ – Petrol #0e3b43 + Koralle #ff6f59. Strenge Draufsicht von oben: Straße mit Halteverbot, LKW-Dach öffnet sich zum Stauplan, Routenfaden zieht wie ein Zugvogel zur neuen Adresse.

## 4. Storyboard-Prompts (3×3, 16:9, 2K, fotorealistisch)

### SB1 – Ein Karton reist mit
A single 16:9 storyboard sheet, 3x3 grid of nine photorealistic film frames with thin warm-cream gutters, consistent hero object in every frame: ONE brown corrugated moving box with a bordeaux-red adhesive tape stripe, a round bordeaux room sticker and a small QR label, handwritten word "KÜCHE" on its side. Colour world strictly warm kraft paper, cream #efe6d6 and deep bordeaux #7a1f2e, soft daylight, shallow depth of field, full-frame camera 35mm, natural film grain. Frame 1: an old sunny kitchen in a German apartment, cupboards still full, the empty flat-packed box leaning against the counter, a website headline area left blank on cream. Frame 2: close-up of a professional mover's hands in modern dark-bordeaux work gloves wrapping cups in white packing paper and placing them upright into the box, box half full. Frame 3: the box closed with bordeaux tape, the mover sticks the round bordeaux room sticker and QR label, the box stands among other boxes each with a different coloured sticker. Frame 4: a mover in modern charcoal work wear carries the box down a stairwell, quilted blue-grey moving blankets wrapped around the banister, camera follows the box. Frame 5: inside an open 7.5-tonne moving truck, view from the tailgate, furniture wrapped in moving blankets, lashing straps tensioned, our box sits in a gap on the upper shelf of the load, glowing slightly as if highlighted. Frame 6: aerial view at dusk of the truck on a city road, a thin bordeaux route line drawn on the road surface leading forward. Frame 7: the box carried through a fresh bright new apartment door, a round bordeaux sticker on the door frame matching the box. Frame 8: the box opened on a new kitchen counter, the same cups now standing in a new white cupboard, warm evening light. Frame 9: the empty flattened box leaning in the hallway, smiling couple with keys in background softly out of focus, cream blank space for a call-to-action. Same box, same tape, same sticker in every frame. No clichés, no cartoon, no text except "KÜCHE" on the box, no logos, no stock-photo smiles to camera, every door has exactly ONE handle.

### SB2 – Abreißkalender, noch 42 Tage
A single 16:9 storyboard sheet, 3x3 grid of nine photorealistic frames with thin dark gutters. Recurring object in every frame: a large tear-off wall calendar block in deep aubergine paper with big mint-green numerals, pinned on a wall, pages being torn off. Colour world strictly deep aubergine #2a1630, soft lilac shadows and mint #7fe0c0 accents, cinematic low-key light, full-frame camera, natural film grain. Frame 1: calendar showing big numeral "42" on an aubergine wall next to a window, a half-empty living room behind, quiet. Frame 2: a hand tears off a page, paper curls in the air, behind it a tablet on a table showing a 3D floor plan of an apartment with furniture outlines in mint lines. Frame 3: numeral "14": a professional mover in modern dark aubergine work wear sets up a temporary no-parking sign with mint border on a German residential street in the morning. Frame 4: numeral "3": stacks of new moving boxes, packing paper and quilted moving blankets in a hallway, each box with a mint tape strip. Frame 5: numeral "1": evening, a person packs a single small box labelled with a mint star: bedsheets, phone charger, coffee, toilet paper, a toolbox. Frame 6: torn-off pages swirling, the calendar now shows "0": wide shot, a furniture lift (ladder lift) reaching a third-floor window, two movers guiding a wrapped sofa, morning light. Frame 7: inside a moving truck, load secured with straps, a mover checks a tablet checklist with mint ticks. Frame 8: new apartment in warm evening light, furniture already assembled, the small mint-star box opened on the bed. Frame 9: calendar on the new wall showing "+14" with a mint circle, an envelope and keys on a sideboard, empty aubergine space for a call-to-action. No clichés, no cartoon, numerals large and clean, no other text, no logos, every door has exactly ONE handle.

### SB3 – Vogelperspektive
A single 16:9 storyboard sheet, 3x3 grid of nine photorealistic frames with thin gutters, all frames shot strictly top-down orthographic from directly above like a drone at 40 metres, never at an angle. Colour world: deep petrol #0e3b43 shadows and asphalt, warm sunlight, coral #ff6f59 accents only on markings and the company truck stripe, crisp 4K aerial photography. Frame 1: top-down of a German inner-city street with trees and parked cars, one house roof, a thin coral line starts at its door. Frame 2: same street, two temporary no-parking signs, an empty kerbside zone marked with coral tape, early morning shadows. Frame 3: a white 7.5-tonne moving truck with a coral stripe parks exactly in the zone, a furniture ladder lift extends toward the roof edge. Frame 4: the truck roof shown as transparent cut-away from above: neatly stacked boxes and blanket-wrapped furniture, lashing straps, one box highlighted in coral. Frame 5: the truck drives on a road between fields, a coral route line on the road behind it, long late-afternoon shadows. Frame 6: the truck on a motorway viewed from above among other vehicles, tiny, coral line continues. Frame 7: top-down of a new modern residential building courtyard, truck parked, movers carrying furniture in a line across the courtyard, small coral dots on the ground leading to the entrance. Frame 8: top-down cut-away floor plan of the new apartment from above, roof removed, furniture placed in rooms, coral dots marking each room. Frame 9: dusk, top-down of the new building, windows glowing warm, the coral route line ends at the door, empty petrol space for a call-to-action. No clichés, no cartoon, no text, no logos, no readable licence plates, consistent truck in every frame.

## 5. Branchendaten (geprüft 26.11.2026, für Diagramme)

Umzüge pro Jahr über Gemeindegrenzen (bpb nach Destatis); Umzüge innerhalb einer Gemeinde nicht gezählt. 2021: gut 1/3 (1,1 Mio.) über Bundesländergrenzen. https://www.bpb.de/themen/migration-integration/regionalprofile/deutschland/548245/binnenmigration-in-deutschland-1991-2022/
- Deutsche Post Adress (Firmenstudie): über 8 Mio. Menschen ziehen jährlich um (~10 %). https://group.dhl.com/de/presse/pressemitteilungen/2024/deutsche-post-adress-analysiert-umzuege.html
- Halteverbot: Abschleppkosten erst ab dem 4. Tag nach Aufstellen (3 volle Tage), BVerwG 24.05.2018, 3 C 25.16. https://www.bverwg.de/pm/2018/33
- Ummelden binnen 2 Wochen nach Einzug (§ 17 BMG).
