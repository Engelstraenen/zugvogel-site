# ZUGVOGEL – Storyboard-Prompts (sjinn Nano Banana 2.1, 16:9, 4K, Unlimited) · 23.11.2026

Fiktive Umzugsfirma ZUGVOGEL Umzüge. Je Variante ein 3x3-Storyboard (9 Screen-Zustände).
Probleme der Umzugsfirmen, die die Seite löst: unklares Volumen/Besichtigung (Rechner, Video-Besichtigung per Handy),
Festpreis statt Rätselraten, Halteverbotszone, Termin/Live-Status am Umzugstag, Personalmangel (Recruiting), Vertrauen (Bewertungen).

## ZVSB-1 „Der Möbellift“ (Nachtblau + Signalorange, filmisch)

ZVSB-1. Website storyboard sheet: one single image, a 3x3 grid of nine 16:9 panels on a near-black background with thin gutters, a small white panel number (1-9) in the top-left corner of each panel. Every panel is a photorealistic, high-end screen state of the same award-winning website for a fictional German moving company called ZUGVOGEL (Umzüge). Cinematic lighting, real photography inside the web design, premium Awwwards-style layout, bold modern grotesk sans-serif typography in white, deep night-blue color world with signal orange as the only accent color. Panel 1: full-screen hero at blue hour, a beautiful old German apartment building facade, all windows dark, an empty street, small top navigation with the word ZUGVOGEL, nothing has happened yet. Panel 3: time-lapse feeling, the lift platform carries a large sofa wrapped tightly in grey moving blankets and stretch film down the ladder, a white moving truck with open tail lift waits below, headline and two rounded buttons "Festpreis in 2 Minuten" and "Video-Besichtigung". Panel 4: motion design chapter: a clean isometric 3D cutaway of an apartment, furniture pieces (sofa, wardrobe, bed, boxes) fly out of the rooms in orange outlines into an isometric truck cargo bay which fills up, a big counter "38 m³" and a price card "Festpreis". Panel 5: a hand holds a modern smartphone filming a living room, glowing orange augmented reality boxes and labels appear around each piece of furniture, headline "Besichtigung per Handy". Panel 6: motion design: a stylised street map in night blue, a red-white German no-parking sign "Halteverbot" drops into place in front of the house, a timeline below with the steps "Antrag", "Schilder", "Umzugstag". Panel 7: careers section, a confident young mover in his early twenties in a navy work uniform carrying a box on his shoulder next to the truck, huge bold kinetic headline "Stark im Team." and an orange button "In 60 Sekunden bewerben". Panel 9: reviews section, large five gold stars filling up, a rating number counting up, slim scrolling marquee bands with short review topics, big orange button "Termin sichern". Ultra sharp, consistent design across all nine panels, no real brand logos, no euro signs.
