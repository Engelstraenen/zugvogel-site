# ZUGVOGEL – Film-Prompts (Drafts gratis, Endrender später MiniMax H3 2K nach OK) · 23.11.2026

Regeln: One-Take, nur First Frame (Wan: + Endbild), ≤15 s, Vorher → Arbeit → Ergebnis, keine Klischees, keine Schrift.
Recherche Möbellift (YouTube „Möbelaufzug Hof an der Saale“): Anhänger mit ausgefahrenen Stützen, Leiter hydraulisch schräg aufgestellt,
Teleskop-Leiter fährt aus, Plattform bleibt waagerecht, Packer belädt am Fenster, Plattform fährt die Leiter ab.

## Film 1 „lift“ (10 s, Wan 3.0 Unlimited 480p, @Image1 = ZV-A, @Image2 = ZV-B)
One continuous shot, no cuts. Blue hour, an old five-storey German apartment building, wet empty street. Fixed layout from left to right: orange furniture lift trailer at the curb (ladder folded flat on the trailer, stabilizer legs on the ground), the white moving truck behind it on the right, the building facade fills the top two thirds.
0-1 s: everything calm, windows dark, street lamps glowing.
1-4 s: the aluminium ladder of the furniture lift slowly tilts up from the trailer by hydraulics, rising diagonally towards the facade.
4-6 s: the ladder telescopes upward section by section, smooth and mechanical, until its top rests just below the sill of a third floor window.
6-8 s: warm light switches on in that window, the window opens inward.
8-10 s: two movers in dark navy work uniforms with grey gloves step to the open window and lean out, one gives a thumbs-up to the street; the empty lift platform starts to climb up the ladder.
Camera: very slow push-in and slight tilt up toward the third floor window, always keeping the ladder top in frame.
Style: photorealistic, cinematic, natural motion, stable architecture.
Avoid: cuts, scene changes, morphing building, bending or melting ladder, extra people on the street, text, logos, readable license plates.

## Film 2 „sofa“ (10 s, MiniMax H3 Unlimited 768p, @Image1 = ZV-C)
One continuous shot, no cuts. Fixed layout: ladder diagonal from bottom left to top left, lift platform with the wrapped sofa near the top edge, white truck with open rear doors and lowered tail lift on the right, one mover in navy at the foot of the ladder.
0-5 s: the platform with the sofa (grey quilted moving blankets, clear stretch film, orange strap) glides smoothly down the ladder, staying perfectly level, camera tilts down with it.
5-7 s: the platform stops at street level next to the mover; he places both gloved hands on the wrapped sofa.
7-10 s: he slides the sofa off the platform onto the lowered tail lift of the truck in one steady push, then rests his hands on top of it. His hands never leave the frame.
Camera locked on the sofa, focus on the sofa at all times, moves with it.
Avoid: cuts, sofa changing shape, platform tilting, floating objects, second sofa, text, logos.

## Film 3 „crew“ (8 s, MiniMax H3 Unlimited 768p, @Image1 = ZV-E)
One continuous shot, no cuts. Young mover in dark navy work uniform at the open rear of the white truck, blue hour, shallow depth of field; he is in the left third, the right half stays calm dark night blue.
0-3 s: he bends his knees, grips the brown moving box on the tail lift with both gloved hands and lifts it in one smooth motion onto his right shoulder.
3-5 s: he straightens up, the box balanced on his shoulder, right hand holding it.
5-8 s: he turns his head to the camera and gives a confident, friendly smile; he stays in the left third.
Camera: slow push-in, focus on his face.
Avoid: cuts, face morphing, extra people, text on clothing, logos, old-fashioned look.
